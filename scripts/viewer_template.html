Rosetta Swadesh Lists

Basic vocabulary from the Rosetta Project's Swadesh lists on the Internet Archive, brought together in one place. Pick a concept to see it across every list, or a language to see its whole list.

    About the licence. The Internet Archive items carry a CC BY 3.0 tag from the Rosetta Project (the 10 items without a tag are left out). The Rosetta Project's 2010 announcement says the lists combine original work by Tim Usher and Paul Whitehouse with outside resources, including Tryon's Comparative Austronesian Dictionary and the Starostin and Peiros databases. The files do not say which form came from which source, so treat the tag as the uploader's statement. Where a concept appears more than once in a list, every form is shown, separated by a bar.
